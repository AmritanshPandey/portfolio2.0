## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Three systemic AI-template tells, each banned by PRODUCT.md/DESIGN.md:
1. A tiny uppercase tracked eyebrow (orange dash + `text-[11px] tracking-[0.18em] uppercase`) above **every** section — plus a second tracked eyebrow per section via `SectionTransition`. PRODUCT.md anti-ref #1 and a brand absolute ban.
2. Animated **gradient text** (`shimmer-accent`, `background-clip:text`) on one word of **every** section title. Brand absolute ban; used systematically, not as a single hero exception.
3. A uniform per-section "transition" connective sentence ("X feeds into Y") — the uniform reflex that reads as AI grammar.

**Deterministic scan:** `detect.mjs` → `[]` (exit 0, clean). The detector matches markup-shaped patterns and did not catch these tells because they live in React `className` composition and CSS — a genuine detector miss, not a false negative on the design.

**Visual overlays:** Browser automation unavailable in this harness; no live overlay. Review is source-based.

## Overall Impression
The dark-default atmosphere and senior restraint are real, and the narrative IA (Work → Approach → Exploration → Impact → About) is sound. But the system contradicts its own freshly-written DESIGN.md in three places at once — per-section eyebrows, gradient-text titles, and ambient orange everywhere — which is exactly what the most slop-sensitive audience (design peers) will clock first. Biggest opportunity: make ember a *rare* voice and kill the templated eyebrow/shimmer scaffolding.

## What's Working
- **Senior, atmospheric dark canvas.** The shader grid + true-gray palette feel composed, not flashy.
- **Proof in the first fold.** Mastercard + "7 years" badges deliver "proof over claims" immediately.
- **Logical narrative IA.** The four-act structure tells a coherent story rather than dumping a grid.
- **Accessibility groundwork.** Thorough `prefers-reduced-motion` handling and dual-theme tokens.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] Templated AI tells on every section.** Per-section tracked eyebrow + gradient-text title word + connective transition sentence. **Why:** directly violates PRODUCT.md anti-reference #1 and brand absolute bans; the design-peer audience reads it as AI grammar. **Fix:** drop the eyebrow as a per-section default (use it once, as a named system, or not at all); replace `shimmer-accent` gradient text with solid-color emphasis via weight/size; cut or load-bear the transition sentences. **Command:** `/impeccable typeset` then `/impeccable quieter`.
- **[P1] Ember is ambient, not decisive.** Orange radial glow baked into every section background + shimmer in every title + two hero blooms means the accent is everywhere softly — the opposite of the One Voice Rule (≤10%). **Why:** dilutes the single accent that should mean "act here." **Fix:** remove ambient orange from section backgrounds; reserve ember for CTAs/active states and at most one atmospheric moment. **Command:** `/impeccable quieter`.
- **[P2] Over-decorated hero / glassmorphism.** Shader grid + 2 blooms + vignette + 4 edge fades + tilt + cursor glare + glass pills + glass paragraph box compete for attention; `backdrop-blur` glass is a brand ban. **Why:** decoration competes with the message and the work. **Fix:** keep one or two atmospheric moves, remove glass on the paragraph, let headline + CTA lead. **Command:** `/impeccable distill`.
- **[P2] Second accent hue in the hero.** The emerald-400 pulsing status dot is an off-system hue. **Why:** breaks the One Voice / no-second-accent rule. **Fix:** make it ember or neutral, or drop it. **Command:** `/impeccable colorize`.
- **[P2] Copy: grammar slip + filler.** "Designing fintech [typing] that scale globally" has a subject/verb mismatch; generic titles ("How I Think") and connective transitions read as scaffolding. **Why:** a senior eye reads grammar slips as unpolished. **Fix:** repair the headline, tighten titles, cut or sharpen transitions. **Command:** `/impeccable clarify`.

## Persona Red Flags

**Dana (Hiring Manager — project persona, from PRODUCT.md):** Scans in ~60s for seniority and the flagship work. The uniform section template makes it hard to tell which project is the headline; the Money20/20 / Agent Pay proof is buried behind a generic "Selected Work" label. The headline grammar slip + typing gimmick risk reading as unpolished to a senior eye.

**Casey (Distracted Mobile User):** The hero loads a WebGL shader grid + two blurred blooms — heavy on a slow connection. Tilt/glare are desktop-only flourishes that add nothing on mobile. Confirm the typing animation and shader degrade gracefully.

**Riley (Stress Tester):** `TypingWord` inside `min-w-[10ch]` — verify long words don't shift layout; confirm the custom cursor never hides over inputs/links and that the tilt card behaves on touch.

## Minor Observations
- Two CTAs in the hero (View work / Resume) is good; ensure "Resume" → `/resume.pdf` opens predictably (new tab / download cue).
- The custom cursor is a nice signature but is a global override; make sure focus-visible rings still read for keyboard users.
- Section backgrounds alternate default/muted well; the soft bottom separator is a tasteful touch.

## Questions to Consider
- What would the homepage look like if ember appeared exactly three times on the first screen — and nowhere else?
- If you deleted every section eyebrow and every shimmer word, would the hierarchy still hold on weight and scale alone? (It should.)
- Which single project is the flagship — and does the layout make that obvious in five seconds?
