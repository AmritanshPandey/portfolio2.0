## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**Does it look AI-generated? Not at first glance — but a design peer will find the fingerprint by the second scroll.**

The first fold is genuinely personal: real photo, handwritten "usually mid-demo" annotation, specific proof strip. Case-study headlines are story-specific ("Mastercard's brand evolved. Its email system hadn't."), and the honest-assessment content (rented PMF, wind-down) is something no template generates.

The tell is the connective tissue: **one micro-label grammar repeated at every altitude**. 46 mono-tracked-uppercase labels on the home page alone (No. 01 / CASE STUDY / TYPE / ROLE / TEAM / IMPACT / DEMO SYSTEMS · MASTERCARD), numbered card scaffolding where the numbers order nothing, seven identical 68px/700 Montserrat one-word section headings (Selected Work / Explorations / Approach / Insights / Trajectory / Advisory / Who I am), and rounded-2xl cards as the default surface (7 in Approach, 5 in Advisory, 6 in About). No single element is banned; the uniform grammar is the 2026 AI fingerprint.

**Deterministic scan**: 113 findings — 98 advisory color-literals (mostly article demo pages and deliberate brand colors in case-study SVGs), 9 font warnings + 1 overused-font (all inside content that *demonstrates* fonts: the typography article and a mock HTML snippet), 5 broken-image warnings (prop-driven `<img>` components; images verified resolving 200 in-browser). **All warning-level hits are false positives.** The structural tells above came from design review, not the scanner.

**Visual overlays**: skipped — the embedded preview pane stops repainting after interaction in this environment, so no reliable user-visible overlay is possible. Fallback: DOM-level evidence quoted above.

## Overall Impression

A real person is visible in this site — the photo annotation, the honest pivot story, the "no invented metrics" discipline. It has also been systematized to the point where every section speaks in the same voice at the same volume. The single biggest opportunity: keep the system in the case studies (where labels are functional) and break it on the home page (where it reads as scaffolding).

## What's Working

- **The human moments land.** The Caveat handwritten annotation on the hero photo is the most memorable pixel on the site. The citizen-safety honest assessment (raised $494K, revenue tracked COVID, market dissolved) is the kind of thing peers screenshot and share.
- **Case-study depth is real.** Options with rejected reasoning, provenance chips, structured reflections — the judgement is visible, which is exactly what template sites can't fake.
- **Token discipline.** One accent, real contrast (body ~95 vs bg ~2.5 lab lightness), dark/light both maintained. The craft substrate is solid.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] The mono-uppercase label grammar saturates the home page.** 46 instances; every card, chip, and section speaks it. **Why**: this exact grammar (mono + tracking + uppercase micro-labels) is the most recognizable AI-portfolio tell of 2025–26; your slop-sensitive peer audience pattern-matches it instantly. **Fix**: on the home page, delete labels where structure already communicates — work cards don't need "No. 01" + "CASE STUDY" + kicker + 4 meta labels; keep the grammar only inside case-study blocks where labels carry data. Target: under 15 instances on home. **Command**: /impeccable quieter (home), then /impeccable polish.
- **[P1] Seven sections, one identical drum beat.** Every h2 is 68px/700 Montserrat one word. **Why**: uniform cadence reads as generated scaffolding and flattens the emotional journey — nothing on the page is louder than anything else. **Fix**: vary altitude per section — let Work open with a full-width statement, drop Advisory/About to a quieter treatment, let one section break the container. **Command**: /impeccable layout.
- **[P2] Montserrat carries everything.** Single default-adjacent geometric for display, body, and UI; only mono + Caveat as contrast. **Why**: zero typographic risk = zero typographic voice; "confident, warm, senior" is not expressed by the default geometric. **Fix**: run the font-selection procedure — keep a workhorse for UI, choose a display or body face on a contrast axis (humanist/serif) picked by voice, not reflex. **Command**: /impeccable typeset.
- **[P2] Advisory is the tallest section on the home page (1493px).** **Why**: the "maybe" audience gets the most vertical real estate, against the site's own "Proof over claims" principle; it also reads salesy, which the brand personality explicitly forbids. **Fix**: compress to a third of its height — one statement, one proof, one CTA. **Command**: /impeccable distill.
- **[P2] Four em dashes in visible copy** (hero proof strip ×2, Explorations intro, About) violate the site's own voice rule. **Fix**: swap to commas/middots — five-minute fix. **Command**: /impeccable clarify.

## Minor Observations

- The Caveat handwritten voice appears exactly once. Two or three more handwritten moments (a margin note on a case-study figure, an arrow on the trajectory) would turn a one-off gimmick into a signature.
- Insights article titles are excellent ("Dating Apps Solved the Wrong Problem") — the strongest copy on the home page, buried at 3,200px.
- Mono for the hero proof strip is defensible (fintech-technical brand) but it's also where two em dashes live; if the strip drops mono, it loses its costume risk entirely.
- Home page total height ~7,200px across 8 sections; consider whether Approach and Trajectory could merge.
