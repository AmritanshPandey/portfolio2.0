#### Anti-Patterns Verdict

Not AI-slop overall — the dark-studio identity, emerald one-voice accent, editorial row indexes, and personal grace notes (polaroid, "usually mid-demo", Off-the-clock) read as a person, not a template. But detector + review agree on pockets:
- Cyan/orange gradient card washes (detector: ai-color-palette on explorations + section blooms) — the generic AI gradient family.
- Numbered eyebrows ("01 — LIVE") on exploration cards — the anti-reference PRODUCT.md names.
- gpt-thin-border-wide-shadow chips (1px border + 32-100px shadows) on wall cards, About glass card, phone frame.
- all-caps body runs (41-53 chars) and repeated 11px body text in case studies.
- False positives: Montserrat 80-98% single-family (deliberate One Family Rule); typography-article specimen fonts (page-scoped by design); phone-frame shadow (device mockup).

CLI scan: 113 findings — 98 advisory color-literal drift, 9 font warnings (specimen article), 5 broken-image warnings, 1 overused-font. Browser overlays ran on / and /work/agent-commerce in the preview tab.

#### Priority Issues

- **[P1] Content visibility is gated on JS animation completing.** Reproduced today: the hero froze mid-timeline leaving a blank first fold; on mobile, eyebrow/intro/CTA/card blocks sit at stylesheet-driven opacity 0 until the timeline runs. Any rAF stall (background-tab open, low-power mode, extension error) = a hiring manager sees a black page. Fix: content visible by default, animate with gsap.from() only, or add a settle-timeout fallback. → /impeccable harden
- **[P1] The first fold lost its proof.** No Money20/20 / Agent Pay / CPO-demo evidence in the hero anymore (violates "Proof over claims"); headline is claim-shaped. No contact affordance before the footer despite "reach out" being the site's conversion goal. → /impeccable shape
- **[P1] Nav bloat + wrong active state.** 7 items incl. the ambiguous Exploration/Gallery/Playground trio; "About" is highlighted on load. Cut to ≤5, fold labs under one label, fix scroll-spy initial state. → /impeccable distill
- **[P2] Explorations band: generic washes + imbalance.** Abstract orange/blue gradients show zero product; carousel arrows for only 2 cards; empty right third; clipped positioned child (detector). Use real product imagery, rebalance. → /impeccable bolder
- **[P2] Case-study micro-type.** Repeated 11px body copy + long all-caps runs. Bump to ≥12.5px, cap uppercase at ≤4 words. → /impeccable typeset

#### Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (recruiter, first visit)**: If the entrance stalls, sees a black page and leaves. Doesn't know what "Playground" vs "Gallery" vs "Exploration" means. Contact requires scrolling to the footer.
**Casey (mobile, distracted)**: Mobile hero blocks are opacity-0 until JS animates; /gallery at 390px rendered footer-only in testing. Tap targets are fine (44px+).
**Priya (hiring manager, 45 seconds)**: First fold gives title + claim but no artifact of seniority (Money20/20, CPO demo) without scrolling; the "reach out" action costs a full-page scroll.

#### Minor Observations

- 98 color literals outside DESIGN.md tokens — systemic drift; tokenize the recurring emerald rgba tints.
- body-level `transition: width` flagged on every page (perf smell; check the global CSS).
- Photo carousel and hero photo painted late/blank in tests — verify lazy-loading behavior in production.
- Footer link glow colors (LinkedIn blue, Dribbble pink) are charming but are technically second accents; keep them scoped to the footer.
